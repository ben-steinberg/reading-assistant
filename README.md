# reading-assistant
This Chrome web extension will rapidly improve your reading skills in a foreign language. While reading a foreign-language website, you can click unknown words to see their translations. The program assesses the website's difficulty and the percentage of words you don't know to calculate your level. You can find your level along with graphs of most frequently missed words on the dashboard. After it has your level, it provides you with articles at a similar level with your most frequently missed words.
